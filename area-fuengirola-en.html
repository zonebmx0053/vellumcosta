The engine of tourism on the Costa del Sol

Fuengirola is one of the most cosmopolitan towns on the Andalusian coast. Its 8-kilometre seafront promenade, the longest in the province, is home to a permanent international community of Nordic and British residents who generate consistent rental demand throughout all 12 months of the year.

The typical Fuengirola tourist values accessibility, amenities and proximity to the beach. Well-equipped and well-managed apartments achieve extraordinary occupancy in peak season and very solid long-stay demand in winter.

Vellum Costa turns this high volume into an advantage for you: we optimise your listings on all platforms, manage every check-in with genuine hospitality and ensure your reviews stay excellent to maximise future bookings.

Fuengirola: the holiday rental market with the most consistent demand on the Costa del Sol

Few towns on the Andalusian coast can match Fuengirola's ability to sustain high occupancy throughout all twelve months of the year. A registered foreign community of over 20,000 residents generates a constant flow of visits from family and friends, while direct rail connections to Málaga and the airport also attract business travellers. For owners, this means fewer gaps in the calendar and less dependency on the summer season.

How to maintain high occupancy in Fuengirola during the off-season

The off-season is where professional managers set themselves apart. In Fuengirola, winter brings genuine long-stay demand: Nordic retirees spending months in the area, remote workers seeking sun and connectivity, and residents needing temporary accommodation during renovations or relocations. An adapted pricing strategy and listings targeting these profiles can maintain solid income throughout the year.

Answers to your questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Fuengirola have such strong holiday rental demand?

Fuengirola has one of the longest seafront promenades in Spain (8km) and a large community of international residents, especially Nordic and British, who regularly visit family and friends. This creates very consistent rental demand throughout all 12 months of the year, with major peaks at Easter, summer and Christmas.

Which areas of Fuengirola offer the best returns?

The most profitable properties are those within 500m of the beach or on the seafront promenade, especially in Fuengirola centre and Los Boliches. Torreblanca has strong long-stay demand. Contact us for a specific analysis of your property.

Do you manage rental apartments in Los Boliches?

Yes. Los Boliches is one of our main management zones in Fuengirola, with strong demand from families and international residents seeking well-equipped apartments near the beach and all amenities.
