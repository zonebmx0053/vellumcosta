Seamless arrivals: the guest experience at the height of the season

July 2026 | Vellum Costa Analysis | 4 min read

In July airports run at full stretch, flights are delayed and many guests arrive tired, hot and after dark. The first hour in the property shapes the whole stay: if getting in is simple and the house is cool, the rest of the week starts on the right foot.

The first hour decides almost everything

A self-access system, using a smart lock or a key safe, removes the problem of out-of-hours arrivals. It does not replace personal service: it complements it. Before travelling, the guest should receive a message with the exact location, where to park, how to get in and a phone number that somebody actually answers.

Guest registration, a legal obligation

Spanish regulations require hosts to collect guests' identity details and report them to the authorities. Doing this digitally, before arrival, avoids waiting at the door and ensures that you comply as an owner. It is one of those formalities the guest barely notices when it is handled well.

A cool home that is easy to understand

In midsummer, thermal comfort is the first thing guests value. A few details make the difference:

  • Leave the blinds down and the property aired before arrival.
  • Simple air-conditioning instructions, in several languages.
  • Cold water in the fridge and a short guide to the house and the area.
  • Clear guidance on the pool, rubbish and community rules.

A fast answer is worth more than a perfect one

In high season, an incident left unanswered for two hours feels like being abandoned. An immediate reply, even just to confirm that the technician is on the way, completely changes the guest's perception. That is why support must be available 24 hours a day, including weekends and public holidays.

"Reviews are earned in August and paid out all year round: every rating this summer influences next summer's bookings."

The long-term effect

Platforms reward the best-rated listings with greater visibility. Looking after the experience during the busiest months is not only a matter of image: it is the foundation of your autumn bookings and of next season.
